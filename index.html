About Our Puppy Yoga Classes

We combine the mental and physical benefits of traditional yoga with the pure happiness of rescue puppies. Our certified instructors guide you through gentle stretches while playful puppies roam, cuddle, and bring endless smiles.

Every session supports local animal shelters and aids in puppy socialization before they find their forever homes.
